# KEYRUSH — Community Guidelines & Fair Play Code

**Effective Date:** October 12, 2026  
**Document Version:** 1.0.0  

---

## 1. Core Principles

KEYRUSH is built for racers, touch-typing speedrunners, keyboard enthusiasts, and competitive gamers. Our community thrives on fair competition, sportsmanship, and personal skill improvement.

---

## 2. Fair Play & Competitive Integrity

1. **Human Dexterity Only:** All typing records must reflect actual human finger input. The use of macros, virtual key generators, automated scripts, or copy-paste injection tools is forbidden.
2. **Speedometer Authenticity:** Racing physics correlate directly to typing velocity. Any tampering with browser timers (`performance.now`) or physics calculation loops is prohibited.
3. **Legitimate Leaderboard Submissions:** Only benchmark scores earned through legitimate gameplay will be recognized in competitive league standings.

---

## 3. Callsign & Avatar Standards

Players are free to customize their pilot identity, provided it adheres to the following standards:
- **No Harassment or Hate Speech:** Callsigns, bios, and avatars must not contain slurs, hate speech, or harassment targeting race, religion, gender, or sexual orientation.
- **No Explicit Imagery:** Avatars must not depict explicit nudity, gore, or graphic violence.
- **No Impersonation:** Do not impersonate staff, tournament officials, or other pilots.

---

## 4. Violations & Appeals

Accounts found violating fair play guidelines may have their public leaderboard visibility revoked. Players wishing to appeal a restriction may file an issue on our official GitHub repository.
